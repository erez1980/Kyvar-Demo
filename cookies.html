COOKIE POLICY · LAST UPDATED 30 AUGUST 2026

Every cookie,
accounted for.

Two local-storage entries keep the site working the way you asked. One analytics cookie, set only if you accept. That is the complete list.

Nothing is stored until you choose. The analytics script is not even downloaded before you accept. Rejecting costs you nothing — every page, diagram and article works exactly the same.

Strictly necessary — always active

These two entries live in your browser's local storage, not in cookies. They are never sent to us or to anyone else; they exist only so the site behaves the way you asked it to.

NameTypePurposeLifetime
kyvar-themeLocal storageRemembers whether you chose light, dark or automatic appearanceUntil you clear it or return to Auto
kyvar-consentLocal storageRecords your cookie decision so we do not ask on every page12 months

Analytics — only after you accept

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 sets the following first-party cookies. If you reject, or simply ignore the banner, none of them are created and the Google script is never loaded.

NameProviderPurposeLifetime
_gaGoogleDistinguishes one visitor from another so visits are not double-counted2 years
_ga_1XD8MVMJ2LGoogleMaintains session state for this specific property2 years

IP anonymisation is enabled. We use this data to see which pages are useful and where visitors come from — not to identify you, build a profile, or serve advertising.

How consent is enforced technically

We implement Google Consent Mode v2 with every storage type denied by default, and we go one step further than the minimum: the Google tag itself is only injected into the page after an explicit acceptance. Withdrawing consent flips the signals back to denied and actively deletes any analytics cookies already present.

Changing your mind

  • Use Cookie settings — here, or in the footer of any page — to reopen the banner and choose again.
  • Your browser can also block or delete cookies for this site: see the privacy or site-settings section of Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.
  • We ask again automatically 12 months after your last choice.
