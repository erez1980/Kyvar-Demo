PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED 30 AUGUST 2026

Privacy at
kyvar.io.

No accounts, no forms, no advertising trackers. One optional analytics tool that does not load until you say yes — and this page explains exactly what happens either way.

The short version: this website sets no cookies until you say yes, has no accounts, no forms and no trackers beyond a single optional analytics tool. If you reject analytics, nothing about your visit is stored on your device or sent to a third-party analytics service.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how KYVAR ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with the marketing website at kyvar.io. It is the data controller for the processing described here, and can be reached at hello@kyvar.io.

The KYVAR product application at app.kyvar.io is a separate, authenticated service governed by the contract and data processing terms agreed with each customer. This policy does not describe the processing of customer data inside that platform.

2. What we collect, and when

Analytics — only if you consent

If you accept analytics in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, approximate location at city level, referring source, device type, and broad interaction events. IP addresses are anonymised. This data is pseudonymous — we do not attempt to identify individual visitors, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. Until you accept, the Google Analytics script is not loaded at all.

Email correspondence

If you email us — for example via a "Start a pilot" link — we receive whatever you choose to send: your name, email address, employer, and the content of your message. We use it solely to respond and to manage the resulting business conversation.

Hosting and delivery logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web server, GitHub processes technical connection data — including IP address, user agent and requested URL — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not have access to these logs.

We do not operate any web forms, user accounts, newsletters, advertising pixels, social media trackers, session recording or heat-mapping on this website.

4. Cookies and local storage

Full detail — every item, its purpose and its lifetime — is in our Cookie Policy. In summary: two entries in your browser's local storage remember your theme preference and your cookie choice, and a Google Analytics cookie is set only if you accept analytics. You can change your choice at any time through Cookie settings.

6. International transfers

Both providers above are US-headquartered, so data may be transferred outside the EEA, the UK and Israel. Google and GitHub (Microsoft) each self-certify under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and offer Standard Contractual Clauses as an additional safeguard. Israel benefits from an EU adequacy decision.

7. How long we keep it

  • Analytics data: retained by Google for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Email correspondence: retained for as long as the business relationship or enquiry is live, and afterwards only as long as needed for legitimate business records.
  • Your cookie choice: stored in your browser for 12 months, after which we ask again.

8. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

If you are in Israel, the Protection of Privacy Law — including Amendment 13 — gives you comparable rights of access and correction, exercisable through the same contact address.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@kyvar.io. We respond within one month.

9. Security

The site is served exclusively over HTTPS, applies a strict Content Security Policy, loads no third-party code beyond the optional analytics tag, and holds no database and no visitor accounts. Security researchers can find our contact details at /.well-known/security.txt.

10. Children

This site addresses IT and security professionals in a business context. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how this site handles personal data, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above. Material changes that affect cookies will also re-trigger the consent banner.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about how KYVAR handles data in general: hello@kyvar.io.
