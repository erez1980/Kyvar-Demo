Copilots suggest. Scripts run. Neither tells you who approved the change, on which system, with whose credentials, or whether it held.

Every change, on the record.

Five steps in the name, one governed path behind them.

  1. KKnowMap the estate

    30 connectors discover cloud, virtual, server, identity and network assets into one record per asset.

  2. YYieldA person decides

    Policy holds risky changes. A named person reads exactly what will run, then approves or stops it.

  3. VVerifyProve it held

    The plan is checked before it runs and the outcome after. If the fix did not hold, KYVAR rolls back.

  4. AActRun as the requester

    Execution on a bounded runtime, with the requester’s identity and only what was approved.

  5. RRecordKeep the evidence

    Every request, decision, command and result in one hash-chained trail, written back to the ticket.

Amber appears only where a person decides. Green appears only where something was verified.
